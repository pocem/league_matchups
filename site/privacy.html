Privacy policy

League Matchups · last updated October 16, 2026

This website

The win probability page runs entirely in your browser. It sets no cookies, has no accounts or analytics, and sends nothing you enter anywhere. The only files it loads are its own, from GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

The Google sign-in

The project's Google Cloud app is used only by the site's owner, to download the public Oracle's Elixir match data files from Google Drive for a daily update. With the owner's own account it makes a temporary copy of a public file, downloads it, and deletes the copy. It reads no other files, stores no Google user data, and shares nothing with anyone. Nobody else signs in to it.
